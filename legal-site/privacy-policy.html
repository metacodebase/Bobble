Privacy Policy - Bobble

Effective date: 28 July 2026

N2 Therapy Australia Pty Ltd trading as Bobble App (ABN 16 633 627 219) operates the Bobble app.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, transfer, retain and delete personal information when you use Bobble and related services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data (name, email, account settings, subscription status).
  • Content data (voice recordings, transcripts, notes, tasks, and AI-generated outputs).
  • Technical data (device type, app version, diagnostics, crash logs, usage events).
  • Support and billing data (support requests and purchase/subscription references).

3. How we use information

  • To provide core app functions like recording, transcription, summaries, and task extraction.
  • To operate your account, subscriptions, and app settings.
  • To improve reliability, performance, security, and user experience.
  • To provide support and respond to enquiries.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. AI processing

Bobble uses automated and AI systems to process voice and text and generate outputs such as summaries and tasks. AI outputs can be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed by you.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We may share data with service providers that support hosting, authentication, AI processing, analytics, billing, and support.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order, or legal process.

6. International processing

Data may be stored or processed in Australia and other countries where our service providers operate.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, security, and dispute resolution.

You can request account or data deletion through the app or by contacting support@bobble.au.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of your personal information and withdraw consent where applicable.

9. Children's privacy

Bobble is not intended for children under 16. If we become aware we collected data in breach of applicable law, we will take steps to delete it.
