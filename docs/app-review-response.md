# App Review response

Hello App Review Team,

We updated Bobble so that, before the microphone permission is requested and before the first voice recording begins, the app displays this disclosure:

“How your recording is processed

Your voice recording is sent to AssemblyAI for transcription. Your transcript and related content are sent to OpenAI to provide the AI features you request.”

The user must tap “Continue” before recording can start. The disclosure also includes a direct “Privacy Policy” link and a “Not now” option that returns without starting a recording.

This disclosure applies to both guest and signed-in users. To verify it as a guest, launch the app, choose “Continue as Guest,” and tap “Start a Bobble.” To verify it while signed in, sign in and tap “Start a Bobble.” On the first recording attempt, the disclosure appears before the system microphone permission prompt and before any audio is recorded.
